# Privacy Policy for DeenSpark – ইসলামিক কুইজ **Last updated**: October 21, 2026 DeenSpark – ইসলামিক কুইজ ("DeenSpark", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when you use our mobile application. ### 1. Zero Data Collection DeenSpark is built as a 100% offline educational quiz application. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or usage telemetry. ### 2. Local Storage Only All application progress, scores, tournament history, and user preferences (such as audio and numeral options) are stored exclusively in local databases (SQLite/Drift and SharedPreferences) located on your personal device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted over any network. You can permanently erase all local progress at any time through the in-app Settings menu ("কুইজ হিস্ট্রি ও স্কোর মুছুন") or by clearing application storage in Android Settings. ### 3. No User Accounts or Registrations You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or social media profile to use DeenSpark. ### 4. No Advertisements & No Third-Party Tracking DeenSpark contains zero advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads), zero analytics tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel), and zero crash reporting beacons (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry). ### 5. Third-Party Libraries Our open-source application dependencies consist solely of standard Flutter framework packages and local SQLite database drivers (`drift`, `sqlite3_flutter_libs`, `path_provider`, `shared_preferences`). None of these libraries initiate network requests or collect telemetry. ### 6. Children's Privacy Because DeenSpark does not collect any personal data whatsoever, our application is safe for users of all ages, including families and children. ### 7. Changes to this Privacy Policy If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions (for instance, if cloud synchronization is introduced in future V2 updates), we will update the "Last updated" date above and provide clear notices within the application. ### 8.
